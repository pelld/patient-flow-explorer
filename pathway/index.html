How a patient chooses where to go

The choice depends on both the health problem and the options the system makes practical.

1

Something happens

Symptoms, injury, distress or a condition getting worse.

→
2

The patient weighs up what to do

What feels right may differ from what a clinician later decides.

How serious does it feel? Can I get an appointment? Is it open now? How far is it? How long will I wait? Did it help last time? Do I know it exists?
→
3

They use a service

Sometimes this is a free choice. Sometimes it is the only realistic option.

GP

I think a GP can help and I can get an appointment.

NHS 111

I am unsure what to do or need advice.

UTC

It is nearby, open and can deal with this.

A&E

It feels serious, or other options are not practical.

999

It feels life-threatening or I cannot get there safely.
